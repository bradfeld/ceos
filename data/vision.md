## Marketing Strategy

**Target Market:**
> Mid-market companies (100–2,000 employees) with dedicated operations or revenue operations teams, beginning with professional services and SaaS companies.

**3 Uniques:**
1. **Speed:** Workflows live in hours, not months — no-code builder with AI-assisted configuration.
2. **Intelligence:** AI that learns your processes and proactively surfaces bottlenecks before they become problems.
3. **Integration:** Connects to your existing stack without ripping and replacing anything.

**Proven Process — The Meridian Method:**
1. **Map** — Document your current workflows and identify your top three bottlenecks
2. **Build** — Configure automated workflows in Compass with our guided onboarding team
3. **Optimize** — Let the AI surface insights and continuously improve your operations

**Guarantee:**
> Your first workflow automated in 30 days or your money back.
