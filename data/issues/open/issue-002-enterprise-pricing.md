# Enterprise pricing model not resonating with prospects

## Identify

**Stated problem:**
> 4 of our last 6 enterprise pilot conversations have stalled after pricing discussion. Prospects say the annual contract requirement is too risky for a first engagement.

**Root cause:**
> Our pricing requires a 12-month commitment upfront. Enterprise buyers want to prove value before committing. We need a lower-friction entry point that lets them start small and expand.

## Discuss

- **Morgan:** I'm hearing this every week. We need a 90-day pilot SKU — something around $10K that converts to annual after success.
- **Jordan:** Makes sense. We need to model the unit economics to make sure it's not a money-loser.
- **Sam:** I can model it this week. Gut says it works if we set the right success criteria for conversion.
